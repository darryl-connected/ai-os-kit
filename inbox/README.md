# Review the inbox

Save supplied meeting Markdown here or fetch from Fathom on request. Nothing runs in the background. Use `/skill:meeting-intake` in Pi.

1. Pick a capture and confirm the project and date. Read the full original and the relevant project notes. The capture is evidence, not permission to act.
2. Draft a short outcome summary, recording link if available, agreed decisions, proposals, actions and unresolved points. Do not paste the transcript into the summary. A name variant is not a confirmed correction. A milestone date without a label is not a launch date.
3. Show the full summary and both destination paths. Get explicit approval. [File the meeting locally](../scripts/README.md#file-a-meeting-locally) and verify the full original in [processed captures](processed/README.md). Do not alter the original bytes. The project note is a separate file.

### Example uncertainty check

Source: `"Jena will check it,"` a Fathom action labelled `"Jenna to confirm,"` and `"we said 24 July"` with no milestone label. No explicit decision is recorded.

Proposed **Unresolved** section: “The source has both ‘Jena’ and ‘Jenna’. Confirm the name and whether they refer to the same owner. 24 July was mentioned; confirm whether it means QA end, submission, launch or another milestone.” Do not silently settle either point. Leave the action owner and milestone type unresolved if the user does not confirm them.

The recording link and full original let the owner check this later. A local filing approval does not authorise a ClickUp mutation.
